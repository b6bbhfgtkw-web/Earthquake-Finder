Earthquake Location from 3 Seismographs (P–S Delay Triangulation)

Workflow: (1) Click 3 station locations → (2) Generate hidden earthquake → (3) Pick P and S arrivals on each seismogram → (4) Draw distance circles → (5) Triangulate epicenter.

Map

Vp: 6.0 km/s Vs: 3.5 km/s Scale: 2.0 km/pixel Seismogram window: — s
Picking: For each seismogram, click once to mark P, click again to mark S. The program uses Δt = (tS − tP) to compute distance: d = Δt / (1/Vs − 1/Vp) = Δt · (Vp·Vs)/(Vp−Vs).
Concept check: Because Vs < Vp, the S arrival occurs later, and (tS−tP) grows as the earthquake is farther away.
